# Research claims

- Split prose into atomic claims and link each to a figure, table, code output, or source.
- Verify population, replication unit, contrast, direction, effect, uncertainty, exclusions, and correction family.
- Distinguish exploratory, descriptive, associational, predictive, mechanistic, and causal language.
- Never report results from an unexecuted notebook. Mark analyses NOT RUN. Do not claim novelty from one unsuccessful literature query.
- Check figures expose donors, sample sizes, exclusions, nulls, and sensitivity results. Reproduce key numbers from the final environment.
- Verify citations support the exact claim and computational outputs are not presented as experimental validation.

Do not approve an untraceable number, hidden donor imbalance, post-hoc threshold, unsupported causal verb, or figure obscuring the biological replicate. Return a claim ledger with evidence location, analysis unit, effect, uncertainty, execution status, required revision, and confidence.
